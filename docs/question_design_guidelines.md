# Pilot annotation guideline (draft)

## Error/question types
- `object_identity`: What object/artifact is shown?
- `attribute`: Visible shape, color, material, pattern, or parts.
- `function`: What is the object used for? Requires appropriate evidence.
- `relation`: Spatial/part relationship or visible interaction.
- `cultural_interpretation`: Regional/cultural meaning; require reliable contextual evidence.

## Evidence type
- `visual`: Directly supported by the image.
- `external_cultural`: Requires trusted external sources or knowledgeable local review.
- `mixed`: Both image and contextual evidence are needed.

## Answerability
- `answerable`: Evidence supports a reliable answer.
- `partially_answerable`: Some claims can be answered, others cannot.
- `unanswerable`: The image/context does not support the requested claim.

## Annotation principles
1. Describe only what evidence supports.
2. Accept equivalent Bangla/English wording when meaning is preserved.
3. Record disagreement; do not silently force a single answer.
4. Separate a visually wrong claim from a culturally unsupported claim.
5. Do not infer a specific region, religion, ritual, maker, or date without evidence.
6. Use two independent reviewers for a subset and adjudicate disagreements.
